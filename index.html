Projects

ASCON-128a crypto accelerator

May – Sep 2026

IEEE SSCS PICO Chipathon 2026, Track A · with R S and Lakshmi

A reusable, plug-and-play encryption block for open-source SoCs. It wraps a 320-bit ASCON-128a core (NIST SP 800-232) behind an AXI4-Lite interface, and encryption and decryption share the same permutation logic.

  • RTL to GDSII with LibreLane on GF180MCU (7-track cells): 0.92 mm² core, 64.95% utilisation.
  • Timing met at 16 MHz across SS, TT and FF corners; 18.11 mW peak power.
  • Zero DRC, LVS and antenna errors; worst static IR drop about 0.1 mV.
  • Checked with NIST Known-Answer Tests, a Cocotb + Python reference model, and gate-level sims with SDF timing.
  • Packaged as an IP-XACT (IEEE 1685-2014) component with Kactus2.
  • 2nd in the proposal round, then a clear "Go" and tapeout approval from the track lead.

Project Kalki

Sep 2025 – Feb 2026

ChipMIT's flagship project · with Megh Giri · demo video

A 4×4 systolic array (16 processing elements) that does the matrix multiplies at the heart of an NPU. We took it from design space exploration on FPGA (HLS) to RTL to a finished layout.

  • Automated flow with Icarus Verilog, Yosys, OpenLane and the SkyWater 130 nm PDK.
  • 400 × 400 µm die, 71.43% core utilisation, DRC/LVS clean.
  • 2.385 mW at 100 MHz, 1.49 pJ per MAC (TT, 25 °C, 1.8 V).
  • Mapped to a real use case: fast blood cancer (ALL) detection for telemedicine, at 25.1 GMAC/s.
  • Won Silicon Sprint 2025; Special Mention at SYNCHRON'25; 2nd at the BIS ECE case study competition.
